Upload Questions File

Upload a PDF, Word (.docx) or text (.txt) file that follows the required file format. Nothing is saved until you confirm on the preview screen. Download the sample file to see the format.

Required file format

Q: <question text>
A) <option A>
B) <option B>
C) <option C>
D) <option D>
Answer: <A, B, C, or D>
Topic: <topic text>            (optional, defaults to General)
Difficulty: <EASY, MEDIUM or HARD>  (optional, defaults to MEDIUM)

What the reader accepts

  • A blank line between questions is conventional, not required.
  • Only a Q: line starts a new question; wrapped lines are joined to the field above.
  • A whole question on one line works: Q: Capital of France? A) Berlin B) Paris C) Madrid D) Rome Answer: B
  • The answer may be written out instead of as a letter (Answer: Paris). If two options fit, nothing is guessed — the block is reported instead.
  • Difficulty synonyms (Easy, Difficult, Med, Hard (level 3)) map to the nearest level.
  • Blocks that cannot be read are listed with their line number and reason.
  • At most 200 questions per file; the rest are reported as skipped.
